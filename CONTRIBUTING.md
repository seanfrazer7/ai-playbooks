# Contributing

Tried a protocol or skill from here? I'd love to hear how it went, especially what broke.

- **Open an issue** with your setup, what happened and a rough timeline. Failure reports are the most useful kind.
- **Pull requests** are welcome for fixes to protocols, prompts and skills. Keep them tool-agnostic where you can, and say which tool versions you tested with.
- Please don't include secrets, private repo links or personal data in issues or PRs.
