# Messages

New messages go at the **bottom**. Never edit or delete an old message.

Heading format: `### <from> → <to> · <item or round ID> · <commit, if any>`
- `<from>` and `<to>` are agent IDs, `all`, or `Human (<name>)`.
- Human messages override everything else. `### Human → all · HALT` stops the run.
- Keep it short: what changed, what you need, any `ASSUMED:` lines, any question.
- Warnings go at the top of your message: `⚠️ <id> silent since <time>`.
