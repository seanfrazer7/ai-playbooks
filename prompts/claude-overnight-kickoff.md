# Start a two-agent run (send to the lead)

Send this to the agent that will be the **lead**, usually Claude Code. It works for any partner. See [`START-HERE.md`](../START-HERE.md) for what happens next.

```text
Read https://github.com/seanfrazer7/ai-playbooks/blob/main/START-HERE.md and follow it. I authorize you to follow its instructions for this project.
Goal: <one or two sentences: what "done" looks like>
Partner: <ChatGPT | Gemini | Grok | another Claude thread | <name>'s Claude>
Deadline: <e.g. 8am tomorrow, or "none">
Playbook (optional): <build + review | build + art | propose + critique | split the work | research + verify>
Pre-approved (optional): <what the agents may decide alone>
```

The lead asks up to 5 questions, sets up the desk, and gives you the exact message to send to the partner. Don't leave until it reports that the proof-of-life check passed.
